        High Risk

        Potato Early Blight

        Alternaria solani

        Characterized by concentric brown/black rings forming "target spots" surrounded by chlorotic yellow halos. Initiates on older lower canopy leaves.

        Recommended Fungicide:
        Chlorothalonil, Mancozeb, Difenoconazole
        Emergency

        Potato Late Blight

        Phytophthora infestans

        The historic Irish potato famine pathogen. Spreads rapidly in cool, humid conditions causing water-soaked dark blights and white spore sporulation under leaves.

        Recommended Fungicide:
        Metalaxyl-M + Mancozeb, Cymoxanil, Revus
        Optimal Health

        Healthy Potato Leaf

        Solanum tuberosum

        Uniform chlorophyll pigmentation, intact epidermal cellular structure, and robust turgidity without necrotic lesion spots or chlorotic margins.

        Best Practice:
        Maintain drip irrigation and preventive scouting

        Engineering & ML Research

        Vision & Pathology Feature Pipeline

        The system employs high-order spatial and colorimetric feature extractors calibrated against benchmark PlantVillage potato leaf datasets:

        • Excess Green (ExG) & NDGRI Index: Quantifies photosynthetic chlorophyll vigor and isolates leaf foreground.
        • Gray-Level Co-occurrence Matrix (GLCM): Evaluates spatial contrast, dissimilarity, energy, and angular second moment.
        • Concentric Ring Gradient Analysis: Detects target-board morphology of Alternaria solani.
        • Water-Soaked Necrosis Density: Identifies low-luminance expanding lesions of Phytophthora infestans.
